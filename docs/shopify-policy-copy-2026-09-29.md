# MetaliQ Art Policy Copy

Use the following wording to align Shopify Admin policies with the storefront.

## Shipping Policy: Free Delivery

### Free delivery on catalogue pieces

Free standard delivery within Pakistan applies to all catalogue pieces ordered and completed through the MetaliQ Art website. Custom, personalized, bespoke, and custom-sized orders are quoted separately, and any delivery charge for those orders is confirmed in writing before payment or production.

Customers must provide a complete and accurate delivery address, an active telephone number, and an available recipient. An order that is returned or remains undelivered because of an incorrect or incomplete address, refused delivery, recipient unavailability, repeated unsuccessful delivery attempts, or another customer-caused delivery failure is not eligible for a refund. Any approved redelivery is subject to a new delivery charge paid by the customer.

## Return and Refund Policy

### Return window for eligible catalogue pieces

Eligible full-price catalogue pieces may be returned within 7 calendar days after delivery. The item must be unused, undamaged, uninstalled, and returned in its original condition with all original protective packaging. Contact MetaliQ Art before sending any item back. An unapproved return may be refused.

### Sale items

Sale, reduced-price, clearance, and promotional-price items are final sale and are not returnable or refundable.

### Custom pieces

Custom, personalized, bespoke, commissioned, and custom-sized pieces are made specifically for the customer and are non-returnable and non-refundable. The only exception is verified transit damage reported within the damage-reporting window with the required evidence.

### Transit damage and evidence

Transit damage must be reported within 3 calendar days after delivery. Keep the artwork and all original packaging while the claim is reviewed.

A continuous unboxing video is required for a transit-damage claim. The video must begin before the parcel is opened and clearly show the sealed parcel, shipping label, external packaging, internal protective packaging, the complete artwork, and the reported damage. The customer must also provide the order number and clear photographs of the artwork, damaged area, packaging, and courier label.

Submitting evidence does not automatically approve a claim. MetaliQ Art will review the evidence and confirm the available resolution after verification.

### Failed or refused delivery

An order that is returned or remains undelivered because of an incorrect or incomplete address, refused delivery, recipient unavailability, repeated unsuccessful delivery attempts, or another customer-caused delivery failure is not eligible for a refund. Any approved redelivery is subject to a new delivery charge paid by the customer.

## Terms of Service: Returns and Delivery

Eligible full-price catalogue pieces may be returned within 7 calendar days after delivery, subject to the Return and Refund Policy. Sale, reduced-price, clearance, and promotional-price items are final sale. Custom, personalized, bespoke, commissioned, and custom-sized pieces are non-returnable and non-refundable except where transit damage is verified under the Return and Refund Policy.

Customers are responsible for providing complete and accurate delivery information and ensuring that an available recipient can receive the parcel. Orders returned or left undelivered because of incorrect or incomplete customer information, refused delivery, recipient unavailability, repeated unsuccessful delivery attempts, or another customer-caused delivery failure are not refundable. Any approved redelivery may require the customer to pay a new delivery charge.

Damage claims must be submitted within 3 calendar days after delivery and must include the evidence required by the Return and Refund Policy, including a continuous unboxing video beginning before the sealed parcel is opened.
